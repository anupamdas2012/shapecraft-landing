From imagination to creation.

Three steps, from the thing in your head to the thing in your hands.

  1. 1

    Imagine it.

    Describe something you want to make — a replacement part, a custom enclosure, or an idea you’ve been thinking about. Start with words, an image, or a sketch.

  2. 2

    Shape it.

    Work with AI to bring your idea to life. Experiment with dimensions, change features, and refine the design until it’s exactly what you want.

  3. 3

    Make it real.

    Download a functional 3D model, ready to prepare for printing. Take something that started as an idea and hold it in your hands.

Parametric, not pre-made

Tune the fit to your printer and use case, not someone else’s.

A downloadable STL is frozen. That doesn’t work when your printer and material choices are different than the original design. Shapecraft leaves every dimension tunable and parametrized for the final part, allowing adjustments as desired for the material and use case.

  • Tune clearances to your printer and material (PLA vs PETG)
  • One model covers many parts — tweak the sizes and print
  • Adjust a finished design without starting over

Built for functional parts, not decoration.

Not a toy generator — a design tool that respects real-world constraints.

✎

Conversational revisions

Ask for a change in plain language — “round the base,” “add a cable slot” — or drop in a reference photo or napkin sketch. Shapecraft updates the model and leaves the rest of your design intact.

⌖

Real-world specs

Name a product it has to fit and Shapecraft looks up the real dimensions, tells you the numbers it used, and builds to them.

▦

Multi-part assemblies

When one piece isn’t enough, it designs the separate parts and composes them so they’re made to work together.

◈

Printable solids

Every part is a single watertight solid — real, closed geometry your slicer can read, not a decorative surface mesh.

⬇

STL & STEP export

Export STL for your slicer, or STEP to carry the model into Fusion, Onshape, or SolidWorks.

◐

Runs in your browser

Describe, preview, and refine the model right in the browser, then share it with a link. No install, no setup.
