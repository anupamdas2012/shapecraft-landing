From a sentence to a printable part

Three steps. No sketches, no constraints, no CAD manual.

  1. 1

    Describe

    Type what you want in plain English — “a snap-fit enclosure for a Raspberry Pi 5.” Shapecraft looks up the real dimensions so it actually fits.

  2. 2

    Generate

    It designs a real parametric model and, when a part needs separate pieces, decomposes it into an assembly — each piece a single watertight solid.

  3. 3

    Refine & export

    Keep chatting to adjust it, step through the revision history, then export STL to print or STEP to keep editing in any CAD tool.

Built for things that have to fit

Not a toy generator — a design tool that respects real-world constraints.

▦

Multi-part assemblies

When one solid won’t do, Shapecraft splits the design into parts, builds each on its own, and composes them back together.

⌖

True-to-spec dimensions

It searches for real product and hardware specs, states the numbers it used, and builds to them — so mounts and cases actually fit.

⟳

Parametric & editable

Every model carries named parameters and a full revision tree. Nudge a value or branch a new direction without starting over.

⬇

STL & STEP export

Download watertight STL for the printer, or editable B-rep STEP to take into Fusion, Onshape, or SolidWorks.

◈

Print-ready by design

Every part is validated as a single manifold solid. If it isn’t watertight, it doesn’t ship — so your slicer stays happy.

◐

Runs in your browser

Describe, preview, and iterate on the model right in the browser. No install, no license dongle, no setup.
